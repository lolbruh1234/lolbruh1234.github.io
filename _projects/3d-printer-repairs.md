## What it is

Running repair log for the shop's 3D printer. Whenever print quality drops or something sounds wrong, this is where the diagnosis and fix gets written up.

## Current state

Printed a Benchy as a calibration test and the extrusion wasn't clean, so pulled the whole toolhead apart to find out why. Opened up the extruder gearbox, checked the drive gears and motor, inspected the wiring connectors, and traced it down to the gear/bearing stack before putting it all back together and remounting the toolhead on the gantry.

## Build log

<div class="log">

<div class="log-entry">
<div class="log-date">October 24, 2026</div>
<h4>Fixed extruder clog — TPU too soft for the MK4S</h4>
<p>Fixed the clog from the MK4S extruder. Took out the extruder assembly to figure out what went wrong — turned out the TPU was too soft and caused the clog. For future reference: take extra care when using TPU in prints.</p>
</div>

<div class="log-entry">
<div class="log-date">October 24, 2026</div>
<h4>Toolhead teardown and reassembly</h4>
<p>Benchy test print came out with rough extrusion, so disassembled the extruder gearbox to inspect the drive gears, motor, and connectors. Found the gear/bearing stack, checked the wiring harness and filament sensor connections, then reassembled the gearbox and remounted the toolhead — with the loadcell and filament sensor board — back onto the gantry.</p>
</div>

<div class="log-entry">
<div class="log-date">October 10, 2026</div>
<h4>Weird noise on extrude, no plastic coming out</h4>
<p>Printer started making a weird grinding noise when trying to extrude and no plastic was coming out at all.</p>
</div>
